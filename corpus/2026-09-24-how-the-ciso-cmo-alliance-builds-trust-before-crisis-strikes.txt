TITLE: How the CISO-CMO Alliance Builds Trust Before Crisis Strikes
SOURCE: https://www.darkreading.com/cybersecurity-operations/how-ciso-cmo-alliance-builds-trust-before-crisis
---
Cybersecurity and brand reputation are inextricably linked. Security and marketing leaders who establish regular touchpoints, develop joint crisis communications plans, and translate security risks into their brand impact position their organizations to significantly outperform those treating security as an operational IT concern.
