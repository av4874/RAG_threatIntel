TITLE: Third Serengeti Sweep Tops 1,400 Arrests as Investigations Mature
SOURCE: https://www.darkreading.com/cybersecurity-operations/third-serengeti-sweep-1400-arrests-investigations-mature
---
Interpol's annual Operation Serengeti, now on its third iteration, led to 1,462 arrests across 20 African countries and the US, recovering $105 million in stolen funds.
